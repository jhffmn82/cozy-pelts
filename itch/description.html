A wholesome family business on the ice.

The seals haul out onto the floes to lounge in the evening sun. Tap one and your hunter runs over and bonks it. Knocked-out seals go on the sled as pelts, and the sled goes up the road to the hut, where the trader pays in coins and never asks where they came from.

Cozy pixel art, a music box, soft little squeals. Seal goes in, pelt comes out.

WHAT'S IN THE CAMP

  • Five clubs, each better than the last: driftwood, a frozen salmon, whale bone, a ship's anchor, and a narwhal tusk that skewers the one behind. Kebab night.
  • Sneak bonks. Napping seals take double damage and give double pelts. Hurt ones scoot for the water, so be quick.
  • Rare seals: golden, platinum, emerald, sapphire, ruby, diamond, rainbow, cosmic and, if your Seal Decoy is convincing enough, holy. They're skittish, and they sell for a small fortune.
  • A husky team that hauls the sled to market by itself once there are enough dogs. Good dogs. They ask no questions.
  • Seal drones lower a hook, pick a seal off the ice and fly it to the wood chipper. Best not to ask about the middle bit.
  • Toss a fish for a seal rush. Mind the gull: it steals off the sled.
  • The Contraption: a candy-striped, loop-the-loop tube that roams the water slurping seals straight into coins. Three tiers, each a little worse for the sea. Nobody knows what the third one does. Find out.
  • Fully voiced: a hunter who is having a lovely time, and a narrator who has seen things.
  • Day and night on the ice, and your camp is saved in your browser.

CONTROLS

  • Tap or click a seal to bonk it, and tap the ice to walk there
  • S or tap the sled: send it up to the hut
  • F toss a fish, Space bonk the nearest seal
  • If things take a turn: WASD run, Space jump, Shift dash, click to shoot where you point (hold to keep firing), right-click to walk there, and 1 2 3, Q or the mouse wheel to switch weapons
  • On a phone or tablet: tap seals, drag on the ice to run, and use the FIRE and JUMP buttons if they appear

THE FINE PRINT

Cartoon violence, pixel blood, and a great many sad little squeals. No real seals were harmed. Several pixel ones were.
