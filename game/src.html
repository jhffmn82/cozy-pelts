Cozy Pelts

A wholesome family business on the ice. Seals haul out to lounge; tap one and your hunter runs over and bonks it.

  • Knocked-out seals go on the sled as pelts. Send the sled up to the hut and the trader pays in coins.
  • Hurt seals scoot for the water. Napping seals take sneak bonks: double damage, double pelts.
  • Save up for drones, a wood chipper and, one day, the contraption.
  • Mind the gull. It steals off the sled.
